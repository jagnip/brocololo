# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Households and families with different dietary needs who plan shared meals and record each person's actual intake.

## Product Purpose

Support household meal planning and individual food logging as distinct, connected tasks.

## Operating Context

Meal Plan manages the household's planned meals. Log records actual intake for a selected person and day using the same underlying plan.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing saved plans, meal editing, food logging, plan switching, and grocery generation.
- Meal Plan and Log are peer navigation destinations, not nested Manage and Track sections.
- This restructuring preserves the existing visual style and components rather than introducing a new visual identity.
- Independent logging without a plan is outside the confirmed scope.
